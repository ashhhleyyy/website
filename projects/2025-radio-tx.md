## About

As part of my time at University Radio York, I was involved in our coverage of the annual [Roses Tournament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roses_Tournament). For this, I built an HLS-based audio streaming system using [next.js](https://nextjs.org), [liquidsoap](https://liquidsoap.info) and [Rust](https://rust-lang.org).

At one point in the weekend, the service run 9 parallel radio streams, and in total was used for 70 streams across the three days.
